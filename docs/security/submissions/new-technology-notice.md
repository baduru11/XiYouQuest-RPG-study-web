# Notice to ITSO: adoption of new technologies (draft, not sent)

ITSO's Adoption of New Technologies Policy asks units adopting new technologies
to email ITSO so the initiative is on record, and to assess risks including
privacy and insecure cloud interfaces. ITSO's Generative AI Usage Guidelines ask
users to avoid entering personal or sensitive data into generative AI tools
unless it is desensitised.

**To:** [ITSO address to confirm; cchelp@ust.hk for general enquiries]
**Subject:** Record of new technology use: XiYouQuest (AI feedback and speech scoring)

Dear ITSO,

For your records, XiYouQuest, a PSC practice web application for HKUST students
and staff, uses:

- Automatic speech recognition, pronunciation scoring and speech synthesis
  from the iFLYTEK Open Platform (Singapore endpoints). Student voice
  recordings are processed for scoring and not stored by the application.
- Large language models reached through OpenRouter to generate practice
  feedback, study plans and companion conversation. Requests are restricted to
  hosts that retain no prompts and do not train on them, and exclude hosts in
  mainland China. Student identifiers are removed from prompts; learner text
  (chat messages, transcripts and scores) is still sent where the feature
  needs it.

A privacy impact assessment and cloud service provider checklists are being
prepared for submission. We would welcome ITSO's advice on the language-model
processing.
